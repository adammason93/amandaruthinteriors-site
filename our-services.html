Our services

Creating PRACTICAL, BEAUTIFUL & TIMELESS interiors is our aspiration.

We create homes that are tailored to the requirements of each client, taking into account the location, brief, personal taste and context.

Offering a seamless, fully managed experience from concept to completion, our turnkey interior design service brings every element of your project together under one roof.

From the initial creative vision through to final installation and styling, every detail is carefully considered, coordinated and expertly delivered. Our service encompasses interior architectural design, spatial planning, lighting and bespoke joinery design, material and finish selection, furniture specification, procurement and project coordination.

We manage the entire process on your behalf, creating a beautifully considered, fully furnished and styled home that is ready to enjoy from the moment you step through the door.

INTERIOR ARCHITECTURE, SPATIAL PLANNING & CONCEPT DESIGN

Building on the detailed brief, we develop the initial concept designs, bringing together the interior architecture, spatial planning and overall design direction to establish a cohesive vision for your home.

Detailed floor plans allow us to explore how each space can be used to its full potential, considering proportion, flow and functionality. Alongside this, we develop the overall aesthetic through considered material palettes, finishes, furniture concepts and key design details.

Our interior architectural design is managed in-house and encompasses spatial planning, lighting design, bespoke joinery, kitchen design and bathroom design. By considering each of these elements collectively from the outset, we ensure the architecture and interiors work seamlessly together.

Every detail is approached with both aesthetics and functionality in mind, creating a considered foundation from which the detailed design can evolve.

FF&E DESIGN & 2D VISUALS

The FF&E stage brings the design to life through the careful selection and specification of furniture, decorative lighting, soft furnishings, artwork, fabrics and finishes. This is where the individual character and atmosphere of your home begins to take shape.

We develop bespoke furniture designs and complete furnishing schemes, carefully considering scale, proportion, materials, textures and finishes to ensure every element works beautifully together and complements the overall interior architecture.

As part of this stage, we present proposed furniture layouts alongside detailed mood boards, fabric and finish samples, and imagery of carefully selected furniture, lighting, artwork and bespoke joinery concepts.

Detailed 2D visual elevations are also presented, allowing you to clearly understand the proposed design and visualise how the individual elements will come together within the finished space.

Collaboration is an important part of this process. We encourage our clients to share their thoughts and feedback throughout, allowing us to refine each detail and ensure the final scheme feels cohesive, beautifully considered and entirely personal to you. Once the design has been fully approved, we progress into the costing and procurement stages.

PROCUREMENT

Our dedicated FF&E and Procurement team manages the entire procurement process in-house, providing a single point of coordination for the many individual elements required to deliver your interior.

Following approval of the costed specification, we place and manage orders across our trusted network of suppliers, manufacturers and specialist makers.

Our team oversees supplier communication, financial administration, order tracking, lead times, logistics and delivery coordination, carefully managing each item in line with the wider project programme.

This meticulous approach ensures the design moves seamlessly from specification into implementation, with every element carefully managed in preparation for the final installation.

DELIVERY, INSTALLATION & FINAL STYLING

Once the site is ready for the final installation, our in-house team coordinates every detail required to bring the completed design to life. We organise and schedule the delivery of furniture, decorative lighting, artwork, soft furnishings and accessories, carefully coordinating suppliers and installation teams to ensure each element arrives at the appropriate stage.

Our team oversees the installation and placement of the specified pieces, ensuring everything is positioned and presented exactly as intended.

We then complete the final styling in-house, dressing each space and adding the carefully considered finishing touches that bring warmth, personality and a sense of completion to your home.

The project concludes with the final handover — revealing a beautifully finished, fully considered home, ready for you and your family to enjoy. From concept to completion, every detail is considered, coordinated and brought together by Amanda Ruth Interiors.
