ABOUT US

INTERIOR DESIGN

Amanda Ruth Interiors is a North East and Yorkshire-based interior design practice specialising in luxury residential interiors throughout the UK, working with private clients and property developers.

We create thoughtfully considered, bespoke interiors that are timeless, elegant and designed around the way our clients live. From full interior architecture and spatial planning through to bespoke furnishings, procurement and installation, we offer a complete design service from concept to completion.

OUR SERVICES

We provide an efficient but in-depth design service, taking each project seamlessly from concept through to completion.

Our turnkey interior design service brings every element of your project together under one roof, encompassing interior architectural design, spatial planning, lighting and bespoke joinery design, material and finish selection, furniture specification, procurement and project coordination.

Our Process

HOW WE WORK

A calm, considered journey from first conversation to the final reveal, with every detail managed on your behalf.

  1. 01

    Consultation & brief

    A home consultation and site survey to understand you, your space and the way you live.

  2. 02

    Concept & design

    Interior architecture, spatial planning and concept design, developed into FF&E schemes and 2D visuals.

  3. 03

    Sourcing & procurement

    A costed specification, then ordering, tracking and coordinating every item through our trusted suppliers.

  4. 04

    Project management

    Design project management to keep each element moving in step with the wider programme.

  5. 05

    Installation & styling

    Delivery, installation and final styling, ready for you to enjoy from the moment you step through the door.

Questions

FREQUENTLY ASKED

See all questions

How much does an interior design project cost?

Every project we take on is bespoke, so our fees depend on the scope and scale of the work and the level of service you would like. Following an initial consultation, we prepare a tailored proposal setting out our recommended approach and fees, so you have a clear picture before anything is agreed.

How long does a project take?

Timescales vary with the size and complexity of each project, the extent of any building works and supplier lead times. As a guide, a project typically takes from a few weeks for a single room to several months for a full home. Once we understand your brief, we will talk you through a realistic programme.

Do you work on single rooms or only whole homes?

Both. Whether it is a single room, a few key spaces or a complete home, every project receives the same care and attention to detail.

Do you work outside Yorkshire?

Yes. We are based in Yorkshire and the North East and work with clients throughout the UK. Please get in touch to discuss your location and project.

What happens at the first consultation?

It is a chance for us to get to know you, your home and the way you live. We talk through your brief, ideas, priorities and budget, take a first look at the space and explain how we would approach the project. From there, we can prepare a tailored proposal.
