ABOUT US

INTERIOR DESIGN

Amanda Ruth Interiors is a North East and Yorkshire-based interior design practice specialising in luxury residential interiors throughout the UK, working with private clients and property developers.

We create thoughtfully considered, bespoke interiors that are timeless, elegant and designed around the way our clients live. From full interior architecture and spatial planning through to bespoke furnishings, procurement and installation, we offer a complete design service from concept to completion.

OUR SERVICES

We provide an efficient but in-depth design service that is split into the following stages taking each project seamlessly from concept through to completion.

  • 01 - HOME CONSULTATION, THE BRIEF & SITE SURVEY
  • 02 - INTERIOR ARCHITECTURE, SPATIAL PLANNING & CONCEPT DESIGN
  • 03 - FF&E DESIGN & 2D VISUALS
  • 04 - TENDER & COSTING
  • 05 - PROCUREMENT
  • 06 - DESIGN PROJECT MANAGEMENT
  • 07 - DELIVERY, INSTALLATION & FINAL STYLING
